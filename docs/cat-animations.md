# A little paws break

The login screen and Care workspace include a fictional animated mascot. Use **Treat the demo cat** for a celebration or **Play peekaboo** to change its pose. Treats are pretend, browser-local interaction only. They do not record feeding visits or change rescue data.

Pause replaces the GIF with its still poster. Reduced motion, hidden tabs, and offscreen placement also stop the animation. The files are local original AI-generated artwork rather than externally embedded GIF services.

Three small, six-frame GIFs live under `public/demo-assets/mews-cat-{wave,peek,celebrate}.gif`, with corresponding static posters and provenance notes. Each is under 200 KB.

## Slides

## Animated cat cursor

On a desktop with a mouse, the same fictional cat follows the pointer and celebrates clicks. Its small dot marks the precise click position. Use **Use normal cursor** at the bottom right, or **Appearance preferences → Cat cursor**, to turn it off. The preference stays on this browser.

Text fields, select controls, maps, draggable targets, and touch devices keep their standard cursors. Tab navigation, leaving the window, and hidden tabs hide the overlay. Reduced motion uses the transparent still poster. The cursor is decorative and never intercepts clicks or changes rescue data.
